# Data sources and credits

- **Villages, tehsils, districts and states**: Local Government Directory (LGD), Ministry of Panchayati Raj, lgdirectory.gov.in. Used under the Government Open Data License – India (GODL).
- **District centre points**: DataMeet, Census 2011 district boundaries, CC BY 2.5 India.
- **Weather**: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). The free API is for non-commercial use. If KisanGuru earns money, check Open-Meteo's terms or take a commercial plan.
- **Mandi prices**: Agmarknet, via data.gov.in (GODL).
- **AI advice and translation**: Claude API (Anthropic).
- **Crop calendars, sowing windows and soil rules** (`backend/KisanGuru.Api/Data/CropCalendars.cs`, `AgroZones.cs`): general guidance written for this app, on the lines of ICAR and state agriculture university packages of practice. Have an agronomist or your KVK review them before relying on them.
